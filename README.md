# Electric_Vehicle-EV-_Market_Analysis
To. Understand the overall landscape of electric vehicles, encompassing both BEVs and PHEVs, to assess the market's size and growth.

 Electric Vehicle Dashboard Insights

A comprehensive HR analytics project focused on the electric vehicle (EV) industry, utilizing **Tableau** and **Excel 2021** to extract key insights and visualize trends in Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs). This project highlights crucial KPIs and visual analytics designed for strategic decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to analyze electric vehicle data to:
- Understand the current landscape and growth trends in EV adoption.
- Differentiate market share between BEVs and PHEVs.
- Identify geographical and manufacturer-specific trends.
- Examine eligibility for Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) incentives.

All insights are presented using interactive Tableau dashboards.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs & Analysis

1. **Total Electric Vehicles**  
   Understand the overall EV market, including BEVs and PHEVs.

2. **Average Electric Range**  
   Assess the efficiency and advancements in EV technology.

3. **Total BEVs and % Share**  
   Analyze the count and dominance of Battery Electric Vehicles.

4. **Total PHEVs and % Share**  
   Understand the adoption and market share of PHEVs.

---

## 📈 Visualizations

1. **Total Vehicles by Model Year (2010 onwards)**  
   *Type: Line/Area Chart*  
   - Showcases adoption growth over the years.

2. **Total Vehicles by State**  
   *Type: Map Chart*  
   - Highlights regional distribution of EVs across the U.S.

3. **Top 10 Vehicle Makes**  
   *Type: Bar Chart*  
   - Identifies dominant EV manufacturers.

4. **CAFV Eligibility Distribution**  
   *Type: Donut/Pie Chart*  
   - Analyzes the proportion of vehicles eligible for fuel incentives.

5. **Top 10 Vehicle Models**  
   *Type: Tree Map*  
   - Showcases consumer preferences based on model popularity.
